Before you start

  1. If you are reading this in an email preview, save the attached file to your computer first, then open the saved file in a web browser such as Edge, Chrome or Firefox.
  2. Nothing on this page is sent anywhere. Your decisions leave it only when you export them, as a file you then send on yourself.

How this review works

Each finding has a proposed verdict already selected. That is a recommendation, not a decision. Change any you disagree with, and add a note wherever your reasoning should be on the record. A TWEAK or MERGE needs a note, because the note is the instruction.

What the export records: your verdict and note on each finding, your overall note and your name, when you opened the page and when you exported, and, so that a review can be told from a sign-off, how long each finding was on screen and whether you changed it. Nothing else.

Your choices are saved as a draft in this browser as you go, so you can close the page and come back. The draft is cleared when you export, or any time with "Clear saved draft".
